# Ball hard negatives

- White shoes and socks are attached to a robot lower body; inspect parent robot geometry and crop
  evidence before accepting a small white candidate.
- Penalty marks are fixed field points and should agree with available point/field evidence.
- Painted line intersections are elongated or line-supported and should not be accepted as balls.
- Duplicate candidates overlap an existing ball strongly.
- A candidate outside known field geometry or without field evidence requires review.
- Small distant balls may be missed; recovery may inspect a bounded region but may not invent
  geometry or bypass human review.
